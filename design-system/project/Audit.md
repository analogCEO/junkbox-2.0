## What's working

- **The logo idea.** A trash can overflowing with junk food is an instant read of the name, and it's funny. The engraved drawing and hand lettering set Junkbox apart from the glossy photo logos most burger places use.
- **Amber.** The logo's amber is already the backdrop of 14 of the 20 product photos on Glovo, so the brand colour is half-established.
- **The menu names.** Samurai Jack, Wild West, Hot Bandolero, Double Cheese-murder and Quadra Kill have real personality.

## What's inconsistent

1. **Two versions of the logo are in use.** The supplied files have the pizza slice and the CAFE & FAST FOOD tagline. On Glovo, the Samurai Jack image shows a version without the pizza on a rounded orange tile. *Fix:* one set of files, traced to vector from the supplied artwork (Logo).
2. **Three ambers.** #F7AF0B in the logo file, #F9B20A behind the photos, #F09C08 on the Glovo tile. *Fix:* `amber` = #F7AF0B.
3. **Only small raster files.** The logo exists as 750 px PNGs, which blur on signs, menus and print. *Fix:* SVG versions of every lockup, plus a horizontal lockup for headers and signs.
4. **Fine detail at small sizes.** The engraving fills in below about 64 px. *Fix:* the avatar version for profile pictures and the wordmark for anything smaller.
5. **Four spellings of the name:** JUNKBOX, JunkBox, Junkbox, JunkBoxBulgaria. *Fix:* "Junkbox" in text.
6. **Six photos are off-style, and one is a liability.** Кесадия Loco, Фентъзи Бокс, Eggs and Bacon and Philly Cheesesteak are stock photos. The item called "Junkbox" uses a stock photo with a visible Dreamstime watermark, showing wings and coleslaw that aren't on the menu. Samurai Jack Меню is a black poster with flames. Кесадия Crazy and Кесадия Веджи share one photo. The Stay Strong photo shows slaw, but the recipe says айсберг and домат. *Fix:* reshoot on amber (Photography).
7. **Menu copy.** The listings mix languages in names ("Wild West Бургер + Fries") and write "new" three ways ((Нов), (Ново), (NEW)). Wild West, Crispy King, Double Cheese-murder and Hot Bandolero list no weight, and the same typos repeat across items. *Fix:* Writing rules and Tags.

## Worth checking

- **Rights to the drawing.** The illustration is in the style of stock engraving clip art. If it came from a stock site or a Canva template, check that the licence allows use in a logo. Many stock licences don't, and that would block registering the logo as a trademark. If it was drawn for Junkbox, there's nothing to do.
- **The lettering font.** Neucha stands in for headlines; if the logo's lettering is a known font, use it instead.
