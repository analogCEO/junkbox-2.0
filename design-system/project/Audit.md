## Findings

**1. The logo says "garbage".** The mark is an engraved, clip-art-style illustration of a burger, fries and cola bursting out of a trash can, under hand-lettered JUNKBOX on a rounded orange tile. The joke reads as "junk = trash", an illustration in that style is very likely licensed stock, so it can't be owned or registered, and its fine lines turn to noise at avatar size. *Fix:* the box mark with the bite (Logo).

**2. Four spellings of one name.** JUNKBOX (logo), JunkBox (Glovo, Instagram display name), Junkbox (Wolt), junkboxbulgaria (handle). *Fix:* "Junkbox" in text, uppercase only in the logo (Name).

**3. Amber is already the brand, by accident.** 14 of 20 product photos are cut-outs on the same saturated amber (#F9B20A). The logo tile is a different orange (#F09C08), and the Glovo cover is black with fire. *Fix:* amber becomes the `amber` token, used everywhere; the logo moves onto it.

**4. Six photos break the system, and one is a liability.** Quesadilla Loco, Фентъзи Бокс, Eggs and Bacon and Philly Cheesesteak are stock photos on wood or plates. The signature item named "Junkbox" uses a stock photo with a visible Dreamstime watermark, showing wings, wedges and coleslaw that aren't on the menu. Samurai Jack Меню is a black poster with flames and the old logo. *Fix:* reshoot in-house on the amber sweep (Photography). Replace the watermarked image first.

**5. Photos that don't match the food.** Кесадия Crazy and Кесадия Веджи use the same photo. The Stay Strong burger photo shows slaw, but the recipe says айсберг and домат. The Samurai cut-out has a visible halo. *Fix:* one real photo per item.

**6. Menu copy is inconsistent.** Names mix languages and suffixes ("Wild West Бургер + Fries", "Кесадия Loco + Картофки"). "New" is written three ways: (Нов), (Ново), (NEW). Descriptions are wrapped in slashes with the weight after them, or have no weight. Wild West, Crispy King, Double Cheese-murder and Hot Bandolero list no weight. Typos repeat across items: Ангьс, чедьр, крьгчета, брош, буого coc, гарнитира, домащен. Coca-Cola reads "(500мл)" in the name and "(330мл)" in the description. *Fix:* the Writing rules and the five Tags.

**7. The names are the best thing here.** Samurai Jack, Wild West, Hot Bandolero, Double Cheese-murder, Quadra Kill, Double Trouble, Crispy King, Stay Strong, Fantasy Box: action-film and gaming references with real personality. The logo, photos and tagline don't carry any of it yet. *Fix:* the character and combo idea runs through the voice, the Character select posts and the bag seal.

**8. The tagline is generic.** "Delicious selection of unmatched street food" could belong to anyone. *Proposed:* "Street food with a fighter's name" in English, "Стрийт фуд с име на боец" in Bulgarian, or no tagline at all, letting the names speak.
