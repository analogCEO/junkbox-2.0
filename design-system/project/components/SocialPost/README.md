# SocialPost

Two Instagram feed templates at 1080 × 1350 (4:5). The preview shows them at 300 px wide; scale everything by 3.6.

**Drop** (amber): the item's in-house photo on the amber sweep, the ink wordmark top-left, the price tag top-right, and the hero name at the bottom in `display-xl` with one mono line of key ingredients and weight. Use it for new items and returning favourites.

**Character select** (ink): one burger per post as a game character. It has 3 to 4 stat bars (сирене, месо, хрупкавост, лютивина, scored out of 10), a counter like "03 / 12" for the series, and the name with one amber word. It runs as a weekly series and as carousels: slide 1 has the stats, slide 2 the photo, slide 3 the ingredients.

- **Provide:** the photo (Drop), the stats (Character select), the name, one line of ingredients, weight and price. The stat scores in the preview are examples; the kitchen sets the real ones.
- **Stories (1080 × 1920):** same layouts, title raised above the bottom 250 px reply bar.
- **Don't:** use flames, smoke, flying leaves or stock food. Don't put more than one display line per post or add a second logo.
