# SocialPost

Three Instagram feed templates at 1080 × 1350 (4:5), previewed at 288 px wide.

- **Item** (amber): the in-house photo on amber, the wordmark centred at the top, the hand-lettered name, one tracked line of key ingredients, and the price. For every menu item and new launch.
- **Announcement** (ink): the white logo large, then one hand-lettered line with the key words in amber. For new items, specials and events.
- **Info** (white): the ink logo, then a hand-lettered headline and one tracked line. For hours, address, holidays and delivery news.

Rules: one hand-lettered headline per post, the logo once, no stock photos, flames or smoke. Stories (1080 × 1920) use the same three layouts with the bottom 250 px left clear. The hours in the Info preview are an example: check them before posting.
