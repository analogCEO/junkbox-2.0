# Content ideas

Post formats and a rollout plan built on the existing logo. They use the three SocialPost templates.

## Regular formats

- **Item of the week** (Item template): one menu item on amber, with the photo and price. Rotate through the whole menu so every item gets its in-house photo.
- **Ново в менюто** (Announcement template): every new item launches on ink with the white logo, then the Item post the next day.
- **What's in the bin?** A reel that opens on the logo's trash can, then cuts to the real burger, fries, cola and pizza being made. It turns the logo into a video intro that can be reused.
- **Домашни туршии** (homemade pickles, sauces): 10 menu items use the homemade pickles. Short clips of making the pickles, burger sauce, garlic mayo and honey mustard show the food is made in-house.
- **Cafe mornings:** the tagline says CAFE, but the cafe side (Eggs and Bacon, coffee if served) barely shows. A morning series makes it visible.
- **Пиротска 61:** the counter, the stamped kraft bag, the amber seal going on. It shows the packaging working.

## Rollout

1. **Week 1:** use `junkbox-avatar.svg` on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and Glovo. Put `junkbox-tile-amber.svg` in Glovo's logo slot and replace the fire cover with an amber banner using the horizontal logo. Fix the name spelling and the menu copy.
2. **Week 2:** reshoot the six off-style photos on amber, the "Junkbox" item first. Weigh the four burgers without weights.
3. **Week 3:** packaging: the ink stamp on kraft, and amber bag seals.
4. **Ongoing:** two feed posts and three or more stories a week, using the three templates.
