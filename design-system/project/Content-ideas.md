# Content ideas

Formats that turn the menu's character names into a recurring social presence. Each uses the SocialPost templates, the amber sweep and the voice rules.

## Series

- **Избери героя (Character select).** One item per week on the ink template, with stat bars for сирене, месо, хрупкавост and лютивина and a counter ("03 / 12"). Carousel: stats, then the amber photo, then the build. Ends with "Кой е твоят main?" to pull comments.
- **Combo moves.** Each combo is a move with its own reel: Double Trouble (two friends, two burgers), Samurai Jack (a split-second slice through the burger in slow motion), Quadra Kill (four burgers vanish from the table in four cuts). New combos follow the pattern: Combo Breaker, Пентакил for five, Final Boss for the biggest build.
- **Cheese-murder cam.** A ten-second overhead cheese pull for Double Cheese-murder, the same framing every time, so it becomes recognisable.
- **Домашни туршии (homemade pickles).** Homemade pickles are in 10 menu items. A short series on making them, the burger sauce, the garlic mayo and the honey mustard. It's proof the food is made here, and it introduces `pickle` as the fresh colour.
- **Пиротска 61.** Street-level clips of the counter, the stamp hitting kraft boxes, and the amber seal going on the bag. They show the packaging system working.

## Launch sequence for the new identity

1. **Week 0:** reshoot the six off-system items on amber, and the "Junkbox" item first. Weigh Wild West, Crispy King, Double Cheese-murder and Hot Bandolero.
2. **Week 1:** swap the avatar to `junkbox-avatar.svg` on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and Glovo. Replace the Glovo cover with an amber banner: wordmark left, three in-house burgers right. Rewrite every listing with the Writing rules.
3. **Week 2:** a reveal post, "Old box, new bite", with the old trash-can logo crossed out by the bite. Start Избери героя.
4. **Week 3:** packaging goes live: stamp, bag seal, wordmark tape. Run a UGC prompt: "Покажи ни хапката" (show us your bite), photos of the first bite, with the best one reposted weekly.
5. **Ongoing:** two feed posts and three or more stories a week. Every new item launches with a Drop post, a Character select card and its own combo name.
