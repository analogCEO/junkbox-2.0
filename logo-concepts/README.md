# New logo concepts

Three directions for a new Junkbox mark, from the brief "a stylised trash can with a stylised burger, or a stylised trash bag with a burger inside". All are flat vector drawings in the brand's ink (#231F20) and amber (#F7AF0B) with white, built to read at 32–48 px (where the current engraving fills in), and paired with the existing hand-lettered JUNKBOX and CAFE & FAST FOOD.

| Concept | Idea |
| --- | --- |
| A · Burger Bin | The bin's lid is the top bun; patty and melting cheese sit between lid and rim. |
| B · Overflow | A bin with its lid knocked off by a burger, fries and cola: the current logo, simplified. |
| C · Junk Bag | A tied black bin bag with a burger bursting through a tear. |

Files per concept: `<concept>.svg` (the mark), `-sticker.svg` (white die-cut outline, for ink grounds and photos), `-stacked.svg` (mark over wordmark and tagline), `-horizontal.svg`. `concepts-board.png` shows all of them side by side.

Rebuild: `python3 tools/new_marks.py logo-concepts`
